How it works

  • Edit existing elements in place. Do not paste new boxes over old content.
  • Keep the original font, size, position, layer, and layout.
  • Every added or changed text run is red. Unchanged text keeps its original color.
  • Fill open fields with the best researched answer or a grounded industry value.
  • Do not add “REC” or “recommended” labels. Put the suggested value directly in the field.
  • Preserve drawings, mockups, factories, origin labels, and other content unless the requested edit changes them.

Review loop

  1. Review the whole pack page by page.
  2. Send the findings first.
  3. Make the safe cleanup in red without waiting for another prompt.
  4. Render and inspect every changed page at high zoom.
  5. Check for clipping, font changes, alignment drift, missing glyphs, page-number mistakes, and footer typos.
  6. Deliver the edited PDF for Milo's final look before it goes to a factory.
