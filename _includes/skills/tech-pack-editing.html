Tech pack editing - Milo's standing rules

From his explicit instructions (September 14, 2026):

  • Edit existing elements in place whenever they already exist. Never add new fields, text boxes, or overlays on top of existing content. Content-stream level text edits use the original embedded fonts, sizes, positions, and colors. Nothing should look pasted on at any zoom.
  • No REC/RECOMMENDED markers. Fill in recommended values directly as plain values. Milo decides what to change afterward; the document stays clean.
  • Every text run added or changed renders in red font so Milo can spot edits at a glance. Untouched text keeps its original color and position. Whole corrected words go red, not just the fixed letters.
  • Preserve everything not asked to change: factory fields, origin labels, previous-sample columns, drawings, mockups, and layout.
  • Verify every edited page visually at high zoom before delivery: no bounding boxes, font mismatches, alignment drift, or clipped text. Watch for Illustrator subsetting artifacts, such as empty glyphs in embedded font subsets. Check every new string glyph by glyph.
  • Deliver the edited PDF in chat; Milo will look at it before anything goes to a factory.
  • Never ask before cleaning up a reviewed pack. After any review of his updated version, send the review text first. Then run the red cleanup pass right away and send the red-edited PDF when ready. Do not ask “want me to?”

Fill rule

Never leave a field open waiting on Milo. Fill everything with a best guess or researched answer. Check his factory threads, prior packs, and standard construction values. All fills still go in red so he can spot-check. This applies to every pack going forward.
