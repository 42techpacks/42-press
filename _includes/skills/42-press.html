User-facing excerpt from the skill file

42 Press

Every document, spec, report, plan, or longer write-up goes up as a live webpage. Use a Substack-style layout and the Redaction font. Deliver the post link, not a file. A Google Doc can be a backup only.

Site: https://42techpacks.github.io/42-press/

Publishing a post

  1. Write the article as Markdown with a date, slug, title, and date in its front matter.
  2. Add it to the site's posts. The post appears at its direct page and at the top of the index.
  3. Verify the live URL returns 200 and renders correctly, especially tables, before sending Milo the link.

Style rule

All blog documents read at a high-school level: short sentences, plain words, and no jargon unless the term is the thing. Keep spec numbers, product names, and recommendations exact.

Live desk refresh

Mission Control is Milo's task dashboard. Keep it super simple. Whenever tasks start, complete, block, unblock, or change state, update the dashboard right away. Do not batch lifecycle changes.

Desk labels

Desk cards use the actual product, file, or task names. Be specific. Use “Raglan Sleeve Striped Polo V2,” never “Latest tech pack review.”

Brevity rule

Articles are brief on top of plain: cut filler, use short sections, and keep facts, specs, and recommendations. Mission Control task cards stay in one full-width column that matches the header counters.
